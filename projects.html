MY PROJECTS AND INTERESTS

From Learning to Practice.

On this page, I have brought together some of the projects I have worked on and the fields that interest me during my university education.

01
REMOTE SENSING

Forest Fire and Vegetation Change Analysis

I studied the changes caused by forest fires in the Seferihisar–Menderes region of İzmir using satellite imagery. By using Sentinel-2 data and spectral indices such as NDVI and NBR, I analyzed vegetation changes before and after the fires.

Sentinel-2 Google Earth Engine NDVI / NBR
02
IMAGE PROCESSING

Digital Image Processing

Using Python, I worked on separating RGB channels, analyzing image histograms, adding noise to images, and applying median filters. These practical exercises helped me observe the results of different image processing techniques.

Python RGB Histogram Filtering
03
WEB GIS

Web GIS and Interactive Systems

I am interested in web-based mapping applications and systems where users can contribute spatial data. As part of my graduation project, I am working on the secure collection, sharing, and management of crowdsourced spatial data. I also develop interactive maps using Leaflet and OpenLayers.

Web GIS Leaflet OpenLayers PostGIS
04
FIELD EXPERIENCE

Fieldwork and Construction Site Experience

During my internships, I had the opportunity to observe practical applications of geomatics engineering in the field. I gained experience with position and elevation measurements, road construction, excavation areas, and the responsibilities of a geomatics engineer on construction sites.

Surveying GPS / GNSS Construction Sites Road Construction
