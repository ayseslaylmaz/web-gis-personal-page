ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Ayşe Sıla Yılmaz.

I am 23 years old and a senior student in the Department of Geomatics Engineering at Hacettepe University.

Throughout my education, I have been exploring different fields of geomatics engineering and working to improve my knowledge and skills.

I am particularly interested in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, spatial data analysis, and web-based mapping applications. I believe it is important to strengthen the knowledge I gain at university through projects and practical applications.

Besides my academic interests, I enjoy discovering new places, gaining different experiences, and learning new things. I created this website to share my work and put what I have learned into practice.
